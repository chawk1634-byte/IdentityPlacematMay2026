Methodology. Vendors evaluated across five dimensions: Data Marketplace (segment breadth, partner ecosystem), Clean Room Technology (native infrastructure vs. third-party reliance), Geographic Coverage (global reach and non-US depth), Identity Coverage (graph scale, determinism, persistence), and Services Capabilities (measurement, analytics, managed services). Ratings are independent analytical judgment. No vendor reviewed or approved them.

Sources. TransUnion/TruAudience: product documentation, 2024 press releases, Neustar acquisition filings. Experian: Marketing Services product pages, public disclosures. ID5: product documentation, IAB Tech Lab submissions. LiveRamp: product docs, Safe Haven and Habu acquisition materials (2024). Epsilon: PeopleCloud documentation, IDC MarketScape 2025. Lotame: product documentation, Panorama ID technical specs.
